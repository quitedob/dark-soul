# Character Classes (角色职业)

## Overview

烬渊 offers **4 starting classes**, each with a unique Chinese cultural identity, playstyle, and talent tree. Players choose one class at the start and can unlock the ability to **switch between classes** at Ember Shrines after Chapter 1.

All classes share the same core combat mechanics (windup/active/recovery, stamina, dodge, lock-on) but differentiate through:
- **Starting stats** (HP, Stamina, Focus)
- **Starting weapons and combat style**
- **Unique talent tree** (天赋技能)
- **Starting spells/prayers**
- **Class-specific passive ability**

---

## Class Summary

| Class | Chinese Name | Role | Combat Style | Weapon | Specialization |
|-------|-------------|------|-------------|--------|----------------|
| **神射手** | Shén Shè Shǒu (Divine Marksman) | Ranged DPS | 羿弓术 (Yi Archery) | Bow + Dagger | Long-range damage, elemental arrows, trap deployment |
| **狂战士** | Kuáng Zhàn Shì (Frenzied Warrior) | Melee Tank/DPS | 刑天斧 (Xíng Tiān Axe) | Dual Battle Axes | Hyper armor, rage mechanics, life-steal |
| **玄法师** | Xuán Fǎ Shī (Mystic Mage) | Caster DPS | 五行术 (Five Elements Arts) | Spell Focus / Staff | Elemental magic, AoE damage, crowd control |
| **祝祷师** | Zhù Dǎo Shī (Invocation Master) | Support/Healer | 天祝术 (Celestial Invocation) | Prayer Beads / Talisman | Healing, buffs, debuffs, spirit summoning |

---

## Unlockable Advanced Classes

After completing Chapter 3, players may discover **hybrid paths** by combining aspects of two classes:

| Hybrid | Combination | Name |
|--------|------------|------|
| 阴阳师 (Yīn Yáng Shī) | 玄法师 + 祝祷师 | Balance of destructive and healing arts |
| 战巫 (War Shaman) | 狂战士 + 祝祷师 | Battle prayers and self-buffing warrior |
| 魔弓手 (Arcane Archer) | 神射手 + 玄法师 | Magic-infused arrows |
| 修罗 (Asura) | 狂战士 + 神射手 | Relentless close-mid range hybrid |
