# 狂战士 (Kuáng Zhàn Shì) — Frenzied Warrior

> *"血未尽，战不休。"*
> *"While blood remains, the battle does not end."*

## Class Identity

The Frenzied Warrior follows the path of **刑天 (Xíng Tiān)** — the headless giant who continued fighting even after decapitation, using his nipples as eyes and his navel as a mouth. These warriors channel pain into power, growing stronger as their health diminishes. Where other classes retreat to heal, the Frenzied Warrior **advances**.

**Playstyle:** High-risk, high-reward melee combat. Excels at trading hits and sustaining through damage. Rewards aggressive play, punishes hesitation.

## Combat Style: 刑天斧 (Xíng Tiān Axe)

The Frenzied Warrior wields **paired battle axes** with a rage-building mechanic:

| Action | Input | Stamina Cost | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Savage Chop** | Light Attack | 18 | Single axe swing (26 dmg). Fast chainable combo (up to 4 hits). |
| **Rending Slam** | Heavy Attack | 36 | Overhead double-axe slam (52 dmg). High stagger. 1.2s windup with hyper armor. |
| **Berserker Rush** | Style Skill (F) | 22 | Sprint forward 8m with axes outstretched, damaging everything in path (18 dmg). |
| **Blood Price** | Special (G) | 0 Stamina, costs 15% current HP | Consume HP to instantly fill Rage meter. 30s cooldown. |
| **War Cry** | Guard + Heavy Attack | 0 | AoE shout (5m) that staggers nearby enemies. 20s cooldown. |
| **Execution** | Guard + Light Attack | 24 | Slow overhead chop (1.5s windup). Deals triple damage to enemies below 25% HP. |

### Rage Mechanic (怒气)

The Frenzied Warrior's core mechanic. A secondary bar (0-100) that fills by:
- Dealing damage: +3 Rage per hit
- Taking damage: +5 Rage per hit
- Killing enemies: +15 Rage per kill
- Blood Price: Instant 100 Rage (costs 15% current HP)

**Rage Consumption:** At 50+ Rage, the warrior enters **狂暴状态 (Frenzy State)** :
- +20% movement speed
- +25% attack damage
- -15% damage taken
- Stamina regeneration +40%
- Rage drains at 8/s during Frenzy
- Frenzy ends when Rage reaches 0

## Class Passive: 愈战愈勇 (Battle-Forged)

Each consecutive hit on the same enemy increases damage dealt to that enemy by 3% (stacks up to 30%, resets after 6s not hitting that enemy).

## Story Integration

The Frenzied Warrior's bloodline traces back to the mortal warriors who worshipped 刑天 as a war god. When 刑天 fell in battle against the Furnace's guardians (Chapter 2 reveals this), his berserker spirit split into fragments that now possess warriors across the realms. The player's Frenzied Warrior carries one such fragment — a source of their power and their curse.

In Chapter 2, encountering the corrupted 刑天 as a boss creates a unique interaction: Frenzied Warrior players can **commune** with his spirit instead of fighting immediately, gaining a special dialogue and an alternative path.

## Chinese Cultural References

| Element | Reference |
|---------|-----------|
| Class name | 狂战士 — "frenzied warrior," from Northern Chinese martial traditions |
| Core myth | 刑天 — from 山海经 (Classic of Mountains and Seas), the ultimate symbol of indomitable will |
| Rage mechanic | 怒气 — based on traditional Chinese concept of battle-fury |
| Talent names | 以乳为目，脐为口 — direct quote from 山海经 describing 刑天's transformation |
| Philosophy | 视死如归 (view death as going home) — warrior's acceptance of mortality |
