# 神射手 (Shén Shè Shǒu) — Divine Marksman

> *"一箭穿云，万里无痕。"*
> *"One arrow pierces the clouds; ten thousand li leave no trace."*

## Class Identity

The Divine Marksman is descended from the lineage of **后羿 (Hòu Yì)** , the legendary archer who shot down nine suns. They channel spiritual energy into their arrows, granting them supernatural accuracy and elemental properties. Unlike Western rangers who rely on stealth, the Divine Marksman fights with **honorable precision** — every shot is deliberate, every arrow carries intent.

**Playstyle:** Spacing-oriented ranged combat. Fragile at close range, devastating at distance. Rewards careful positioning, timing, and shot selection over button-mashing.

## Combat Style: 羿弓术 (Yì Archery)

The Divine Marksman uses a unique combat style that replaces melee attacks with archery mechanics:

| Action | Input | Stamina Cost | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Quick Shot** | Light Attack | 12 | Fast arrow, low damage (18). Good for chip damage. |
| **Power Shot** | Heavy Attack | 28 | Charged arrow (0.8s windup), high damage (42). Can hold charge for 3s. |
| **Evade Shot** | Dodge + Light Attack | 26 | Backward leap that fires an arrow mid-jump (14 dmg). Creates distance. |
| **Elemental Arrow** | Style Skill (F) | Focus varies | Imbues next 3 arrows with selected element |
| **Arrow Rain** | Special (G while locked on) | 40 Focus | Fires a volley into the air that rains down on locked target (8 arrows × 8 dmg) |
| **Dagger Slash** | Guard + Light Attack | 10 | Quick melee slash with off-hand dagger (12 dmg). Emergency close-range tool. |

### Elemental Arrows (Focus-cost toggle)

| Element | Focus/Arrow | Effect |
|---------|------------|--------|
| **火矢 (Fire Arrow)** | 8 | +25% damage, leaves burning ground (3s, 5 dmg/s) |
| **冰矢 (Ice Arrow)** | 8 | Slows enemy 30% for 4s, builds freeze (at 3 stacks, enemy frozen 2s) |
| **雷矢 (Lightning Arrow)** | 10 | Chains to 2 nearby enemies for 40% damage |
| **灵矢 (Spirit Arrow)** | 5 | Homing arrow — curves toward locked target, can hit behind partial cover |

## Class Passive: 灵矢感知 (Spirit Arrow Sense)

Nearby hidden enemies, traps, and breakable walls emit a faint glow visible only to the Divine Marksman. Range: 12m. Helps with trap detection and secret-finding.

## Story Integration

The Divine Marksman's ancestor was one of the mortal warriors who fought alongside the Soul-Forgers during the Shattering. Their bow was shattered in the battle; the fragments were scattered across the five realms. Collecting all fragments (hidden throughout chapters) unlocks the legendary weapon **落日弓 (Sun-Falling Bow)** — the very bow used by 后羿 in myth.
