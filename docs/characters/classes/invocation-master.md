# 祝祷师 (Zhù Dǎo Shī) — Invocation Master

> *"一念慈悲，万灵归心。"*
> *"One thought of compassion; ten thousand spirits return to the heart."*

## Class Identity

The Invocation Master practices **天祝术 (Celestial Invocation)** — the art of calling upon higher powers and ancestral spirits for aid. Rooted in Chinese Buddhist and folk religious traditions, Invocation Masters are not priests serving a single deity but **mediators** between the mortal world and the spirit realm. They heal the wounded, bless the righteous, curse the wicked, and summon spirit allies.

**Playstyle:** Support/utility with offensive options. Invocation Masters can heal (the only class with reliable healing), buff allies (if co-op), debuff enemies, and summon spirit entities. Low direct damage but high survivability and utility.

## Combat Style: 天祝术 (Celestial Invocation)

The Invocation Master uses **念珠 (Prayer Beads)** and **符纸 (Talisman Papers)** to channel spiritual power:

| Action | Input | Focus Cost | Description |
|--------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **慈悲之光 (Light of Compassion)** | Light Attack | 15 | Heal self or targeted ally for 22 HP. Long range (15m) if targeted. |
| **业火符 (Karmic Fire Talisman)** | Heavy Attack | 20 | Throw a talisman that explodes on contact (24 dmg, 4m AoE). Applies "Karmic Debt" debuff. |
| **召唤灵体 (Spirit Summon)** | Style Skill (F) | 30 + 20% max Focus reserved | Summon a spirit ally. Type depends on equipped 灵符 (Spirit Talisman). Reserved Focus returns when spirit is dismissed. |
| **净化解咒 (Purification)** | Special (G) | 25 | Cleanse all negative status effects from self. 30s cooldown. |
| **往生咒 (Rebirth Mantra)** | Guard | Channel (8 Focus/s) | Create a 5m aura that slowly heals all allies (6 HP/s). Caster cannot move while channeling. |
| **超度 (Soul Release)** | Guard + Heavy Attack | 40 | Release stored "karmic energy" as a damaging wave (dmg = stored karma × 1.5). Resets karma to 0. |

### Karmic Debt Mechanic (业力)

A unique resource for Invocation Masters:

- **Karmic Fire Talisman** and certain prayers apply stacks of "Karmic Debt" to enemies (max 10 stacks)
- Each stack: enemy deals -2% damage, takes +2% damage from all sources
- At 10 stacks, enemy becomes **超度状态 (Marked for Release)** — takes double damage from Soul Release
- Stacks decay at 1 per 8 seconds if not refreshed
- Certain enemies (bosses, divine beings) have stack caps (bosses: 5 max)

## Class Passive: 因果报应 (Karmic Retribution)

When the Invocation Master takes damage, the attacker receives 1 stack of Karmic Debt. This makes trading hits with an Invocation Master increasingly unfavorable.

## Story Integration

祝祷之术 (The Art of Invocation) predates the Celestial Furnace itself. Before the cosmic cycle was mechanized, mortal shamans and spirit-mediums guided souls to the afterlife through ritual and prayer. When the Furnace was built, many believed it made invocation obsolete — but the Shattering proved otherwise. With the cycle broken, Invocation Masters are the only ones who can still grant souls a measure of peace.

In Chapter 5, the Invocation Master's ability to communicate with lingering souls provides unique dialogue options with the drifting spirits in the Throne of Ashes. They may learn secrets about 烛阴's original discovery that no other class can access.
