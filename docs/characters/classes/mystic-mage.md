# 玄法师 (Xuán Fǎ Shī) — Mystic Mage

> *"道生一，一生二，二生三，三生万物。"*
> *"The Dao gives birth to One, One to Two, Two to Three, Three to all things."*

## Class Identity

The Mystic Mage is a practitioner of **玄门道法 (Mystic Gate Dao Arts)** — the esoteric magical tradition derived from Taoist cultivation practices. They do not merely cast spells; they manipulate the fundamental **五行 (Five Elements)** — Fire, Water, Wood, Metal, Earth — each with distinct tactical applications.

**Playstyle:** Strategic caster. Tools for every situation but fragile. Rewards elemental matching, positioning, and Focus management. Punishes button-mashing and poor resource management.

## Combat Style: 五行术 (Five Elements Arts)

The Mystic Mage replaces melee weapons with a **法印 (Spell Seal)** — a floating sigil that projects magical attacks:

| Action | Input | Focus Cost | Description |
|--------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **灵弹 (Spirit Bolt)** | Light Attack | 8 | Fast magic projectile (16 dmg). No cooldown. Basic spam spell. |
| **元素爆发 (Elemental Burst)** | Heavy Attack | 25 | Charged elemental attack based on active element. 1.0s cast. |
| **元素切换 (Element Shift)** | Style Skill (F) | 0 | Cycles through the five elements (Fire → Water → Wood → Metal → Earth → Fire) |
| **法阵 (Magic Formation)** | Special (G) | 35 | Deploys a stationary magic circle (4m radius, 8s duration). Effect depends on active element. |
| **瞬移 (Flash Step)** | Dodge (during cast recovery) | 15 Focus + 20 Stamina | Short-range teleport instead of roll. Passes through enemies. |
| **法盾 (Spell Shield)** | Guard | 5 Focus/s | Absorbs 70% of incoming damage as Focus drain instead of HP loss |

## Class Passive: 灵气感知 (Spirit Energy Perception)

The Mystic Mage can see enemy Focus bars and elemental weaknesses through a faint aura visible around each enemy. Elemental weakness types glow more brightly. This allows strategic element selection before engagement.

## Story Integration

玄法之道 (The Way of Mystic Arts) was the first magic system developed by mortals who studied the Celestial Furnace's operation. The original 玄法师 were Furnace-Keeper initiates who learned to channel Ember-energy without being burned. Their tradition survived the Shattering in scattered scrolls and oral teachings.

In Chapter 3, the Jade-Green Forest's illusions are rooted in Wood-element magic — Mystic Mages can use their Fire element to see through certain illusions that other classes must solve through puzzles.
