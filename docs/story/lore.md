# 烬渊 — World Lore (世界观)

## Cosmology

### The Three Realms (Original State)

Before the Shattering, existence was ordered into three interconnected realms:

| Realm | Chinese | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Heaven** | 天界 (Tiān Jiè) | Realm of immortals, divine beasts, and celestial bureaucracy. The Celestial Furnace floated at its center. |
| **Mortal Realm** | 人间 (Rén Jiān) | The living world of humans, animals, spirits, and cultivation. Souls were born here and returned here. |
| **Underworld** | 冥界 (Míng Jiè) | The realm of the dead. Souls traveled here to be judged, purified, and sent back to the Mortal Realm through the Celestial Furnace. |

### The Celestial Furnace (天之炉)

The **天之炉 (Tiān Zhī Lú)** was not literally a furnace — it was a cosmic mechanism that took the form of an immense, star-burning brazier suspended at the intersection of all three realms. Its functions:

1. **Purification:** Burned away the accumulated karma and memory of dead souls
2. **Reforging:** Compressed purified soul-essence into new Embers
3. **Distribution:** Released Embers into the cosmic current to become new souls

The Furnace was tended by the **铸魂者 (Soul-Forgers)** — twelve immortal beings created at the dawn of existence specifically for this purpose. 烛阴 was their leader and most powerful member.

### The Shattering (大破碎)

Approximately 500 years before the game begins:

1. 烛阴 announced to the other Soul-Forgers that the Ember-light was dimming
2. He proposed **永恒锻法 (The Eternal Forging)** — a plan to concentrate all Embers into a single, perfect, unchanging soul-reality
3. The other eleven Soul-Forgers refused, arguing that the cycle was sacred
4. 烛阴 attempted to seize the Furnace by force; the eleven opposed him
5. The resulting battle shattered the Furnace into **five fragments**
6. Eleven Soul-Forgers died or vanished; only one survived (云游道人, later The Wandering Sage)
7. 烛阴 was bound in chains of solidified Ember and cast into the Furnace's core

### The Five Fragments

Each fragment of the Furnace retained a portion of its original power and fell into a different realm:

| Fragment | Chinese Name | Fell Into | Corrupted By |
|----------|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| 始烬 (First Ember) | 灵墟碎片 | Spirit Guardian Temple | Residual guardian programming |
| 战烬 (War Ember) | 血铁碎片 | Iron Howl Pass | Eternal battle trauma |
| 幻烬 (Illusion Ember) | 玉障碎片 | Jade-Green Forest | Nine-Tails' dream-weaving |
| 天烬 (Sky Ember) | 天崩碎片 | Cloud Zenith City | Cultivator hubris and ambition |
| 终烬 (Final Ember) | 烬座核心 | Throne of Ashes | 烛阴 himself |

## Factions

### 铸魂者 (Soul-Forgers)
The twelve original guardians of the Furnace. Each embodied a different aspect of the cosmic cycle:

1. **烛阴 (Torch Dragon)** — Light and Time (Leader, antagonist)
2. **云游 (Cloud Wanderer)** — Journey and Guidance (Survivor, guide NPC)
3. **铸星 (Star-Forger)** — Creation (Deceased)
4. **断念 (Thought-Breaker)** — Release and Forgetting (Deceased)
5. **归尘 (Dust-Returner)** — Death and Acceptance (Deceased)
6. **织命 (Fate-Weaver)** — Destiny (Deceased)
7. **守门 (Gate-Keeper)** — Thresholds (Deceased)
8. **量罪 (Sin-Measurer)** — Judgment (Deceased)
9. **燃灯 (Lamp-Lighter)** — Hope (Deceased)
10. **镇魂 (Soul-Pacifier)** — Peace (Deceased)
11. **循环 (Cycle-Turner)** — Motion and Change (Deceased)
12. **寂灭 (Silence-Bringer)** — Endings (Deceased)

### 烬裔 (Ember Scions)
Rare beings born with a fragment of original Furnace-fire in their soul. Cannot be created — only born at random when a particular reincarnation aligns with residual Ember-light. The player character is the last known Ember Scion.

**Known Abilities of Ember Scions:**
- Absorb Embers from defeated enemies to grow stronger
- Pass between realms without being corrupted
- Perceive the "true nature" of Ember-warped beings
- Kindle checkpoints (烬龛 / Ember Shrines)

### 失魂者 (Lost Souls)
The vast majority of beings in the current world. When the Furnace shattered, the soul-recycling process stopped. Souls that die now cannot pass on — they become Lost Souls, gradually losing their memories and identity until they fade to nothing. Some go mad; some become hostile; some simply... stop.

### 堕仙 (Fallen Immortals)
Cultivators who were in the process of ascending to immortality when the Furnace broke. The interruption froze their transformation, leaving them in grotesque half-states. They retain immense power but have lost their sanity. Found primarily in Chapter 4.

## Key Concepts

### 烬 (Embers)
The fundamental "currency" of the universe. An Ember is a purified soul-essence — the raw material from which new souls are forged. After the Shattering, Embers became scarce. They function as both:
- **Experience points:** Absorbing Embers strengthens an Ember Scion
- **Currency:** Used at 烬龛 (Ember Shrines) for upgrades and forging

### 烬龛 (Ember Shrines)
Checkpoint/bonfire equivalents. Small braziers containing a spark of original Furnace-fire. Functions:
- **Rest:** Restore HP, Stamina, Focus; respawn enemies
- **Kindle:** Activate as respawn point
- **Forge:** Spend Embers on upgrades (Vitality, weapon reinforcement, talent unlocks)
- **Travel:** After Chapter 2, shrines connect to other discovered shrines

### 魂器 (Soul Vessels)
Special equipment that grows with the player. Each chapter's boss drops a Soul Vessel — a unique item that can be equipped to gain abilities themed after that boss. Soul Vessels can be upgraded with Embers.

### 道行 (Cultivation Level)
The player's overall power level. Increases by absorbing Embers. Each level grants:
- +5 max HP
- +3 max Stamina (every 2 levels)
- +2 max Focus (every 2 levels)
- 1 Talent Point (every level)

### 轮回印 (Samsara Seal)
The mark that identifies an Ember Scion. Visible as a faint, glowing brand on the character's chest/forehead. Opens shortcuts, activates shrines, and allows realm traversal.

## Calendar & Time

The game's world does not follow a standard calendar. Time is measured by:
- **烬相 (Ember Phase):** The brightness of Ember-light in the sky, which waxes and wanes unpredictably
- **轮回数 (Cycle Count):** How many times a soul has reincarnated (only Soul-Forgers can perceive this)

## Languages & Writing

- **天篆 (Celestial Seal Script):** The writing of immortals, found in Chapter 4 and 5
- **冥文 (Underworld Script):** Used for Ember Shrine inscriptions and boss names
- **凡文 (Mortal Script):** Standard Chinese characters used by human NPCs and item descriptions

## The Wandering Sage's Secret

**Spoiler — revealed in Chapter 4:**

云游道人 (The Wandering Sage) was not merely present at the Shattering — he cast the deciding vote. When the eleven Soul-Forgers opposed 烛阴, 云游 was the one who hesitated. His aspect is "Journey and Guidance" — he could not accept that the cycle was perfect, nor that 烛阴's eternal stasis was the answer. His indecision created the opening 烛阴 exploited.

After the Shattering, 云游 spent centuries wandering the broken realms, observing how beings adapted to the new reality. He concluded that **both sides were wrong**: the old cycle was slowly failing, and 烛阴's solution was a different kind of death. The only answer is for someone new — an Ember Scion — to enter the Throne and choose freely.

He guides you not to ensure a particular outcome, but because you represent the one thing neither side had: **genuine choice**. You were born after the Shattering. You owe allegiance to neither the old order nor 烛阴's rebellion.
