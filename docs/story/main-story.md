# 烬渊 — Main Story (主线剧情)

## Prologue: The Shattering of the Celestial Furnace

In the beginning, the world operated on a cosmic cycle governed by the **天之炉 (Celestial Furnace)** — a divine construct that sat at the nexus of all realms, burning the souls of the dead into pure 烬 (Embers). These Embers would rise into the heavens, cool into starlight, and eventually rain back down as newborn souls. Life, death, rebirth — an eternal, balanced cycle overseen by the **铸魂者 (Soul-Forgers)**, immortal beings tasked with tending the Furnace.

For ten thousand years, the cycle held.

Then came **烛阴 (Zhú Yīn)** — the Torch Dragon, the most powerful of the Soul-Forgers. He gazed into the Furnace for an eternity and saw something in its flames that broke his faith: the Embers of every soul that had ever existed were slowly dimming. The universe was running out of light. Each reincarnation burned a little less bright than the last. Given enough time, all souls would fade to nothing.

烛阴 concluded that the cycle itself was the flaw — a slow, grinding entropy disguised as eternity. He resolved to **break the Furnace**, gather all remaining Embers, and reforge existence into a single, eternal, unchanging moment where nothing would ever fade again.

Nine Soul-Forgers opposed him. 寂灭 accepted his diagnosis but rejected forced stasis, while 云游 reversed the Furnace current to stop the ritual. The collision shattered the Celestial Furnace into five fragments, each hurled into a different region. Nine Soul-Forgers died containing the breach. 烛阴 fused with the final fragment and was bound in the **烬座 (Throne of Ashes)**; 云游 and 寂灭 survived with different forms of guilt.

Now, five centuries later, the five fractured regions have become twisted, isolated nightmares. Souls no longer reincarnate — they wander as **失魂者 (Lost Souls)**, gradually fading into nothing. The dead pile up in the underworld with no passage forward. The living realm decays as no new souls arrive.

You are the last **烬裔 (Ember Scion)** — an unfinished successor forged during the Shattering from unassigned soul-light, the nine fallen Soul-Forgers' seals, and the First Ember. You awaken with no personal past and an incomplete 轮回印 that will disperse unless it is stabilized by the other fragments. What begins as survival becomes a question of identity: will you merely execute the dead guardians' repair plan, inherit 烛阴's answer, or choose a future neither side designed?

For the authoritative chapter-to-chapter causality, evidence, NPC transfers, and story flags, see [chapter-bridge-map.md](chapter-bridge-map.md).

## Chapter 1: 灵墟·觉醒 (Spirit Ruins · Awakening)

### "The dead do not rest. They wait."

You awaken inside the **守炉殿 (Furnace-Keeper's Shrine)** — an abandoned temple complex that once served as the gateway between the mortal world and the Celestial Furnace. The shrine's guardian, a massive stone-and-ember construct called **巨阙 (Jù Quē)**, still patrols the inner sanctum, mindlessly performing its ancient duty.

The temple has been overrun by **失魂士兵 (Lost Soul Soldiers)** — the husks of warriors who died trying to reach the shrine. A mysterious voice — later revealed to be **云游道人 (The Wandering Sage)**, one of the few surviving Soul-Forgers — guides you through the temple, teaching you the basics of combat, Ember collection, and survival.

**Key Story Beats:**
- Learn the world's rules through environmental storytelling
- Discover the first Furnace fragment in the Guardian's chamber
- Defeat 巨阙, proving you are a true Ember Scion
- The Wandering Sage reveals the existence of the other four fragments
- Use the Keeper Rune to either release 巨阙 from duty or preserve its core as the shrine's new guardian
- The First Ember reveals a blood-stained military coordinate and opens the road to 铁啸关
- A vision shows 烛阴 stirring in his throne, aware of your awakening

**Tone:** Mystery, loneliness, discovery. The tutorial teaches through atmosphere — ancient murals, scattered journal entries, and the gentle guidance of the Wandering Sage.

## Chapter 2: 血铁·战歌 (Blood & Iron · Warsong)

### "War never ended. It only forgot why it began."

The second fragment lies within the **铁啸关 (Iron Howl Pass)** — a mountain fortress where an eternal battle rages between two armies that no longer remember their cause. The fragment's power has trapped them in a cycle of endless war: soldiers who die at dawn rise again at dusk, their spirits too corrupted by Ember-madness to pass on.

The fortress is ruled by **血将军·刑天 (Blood General · Xíng Tiān)** — a headless giant who was once the mightiest warrior of a forgotten dynasty. After losing his head in battle against the Celestial Furnace's guardians, his rage and the fragment's power fused him into an immortal berserker. He now leads both armies simultaneously, craving only worthy opponents.

**Key Story Beats:**
- Navigate the war-torn fortress, choosing which army's territory to cross
- Free **铁心**, the last lucid spirit-smith, who unlocks forging and identifies the military soul-seal hidden in 刑天's body
- Confront 刑天 in the Fortress Arena as both armies watch
- Choose to grant 刑天 an honorable release or absorb his rage before claiming the War Ember
- 刑天's final command seal points to a witness hidden in 翠微林 who remembers the Shattering

**Tone:** Martial, brutal, honor-bound tragedy. The enemy soldiers are victims as much as obstacles.

## Chapter 3: 玉障·迷心 (Jade Veil · Lost Mind)

### "What you see is what was, not what is."

The third fragment lies in **翠微林 (Jade-Green Forest)** — a once-sacred grove now shrouded in permanent, shimmering mist. The fragment's power has merged with the forest's natural energy, creating layered illusions that trap travelers in their own memories and desires.

The forest is ruled by **玉面狐·九尾 (Jade-Faced Fox · Nine-Tails)** — a nine-tailed fox spirit who was once the forest's guardian deity. Corrupted by the fragment, she now feeds on the memories of those who enter her domain, weaving elaborate dream-realities from their past. She is not evil — she genuinely believes she is giving her victims eternal happiness by trapping them in perfected versions of their memories.

**Key Story Beats:**
- Navigate illusion-shifting environments where reality changes
- Encounter borrowed memories and initially mistake them for your own past lives
- Free **忆姬**, keeper of the forest's true records, who proves the memories belong to souls trapped in the fragments
- Free trapped spirits by breaking their illusion prisons
- Confront Nine-Tails in her Moonlit Pavilion, where she takes the form of someone remembered by the fragments
- Learn that you were made during the Shattering rather than naturally born, and choose to redeem Nine-Tails or seal her remaining spirit
- Receive the **天门残印 (Broken Heaven-Gate Seal)**, the only key that can open the path to Cloud Zenith City

**Tone:** Elegant, deceptive, melancholy. Beauty hides horror. The "enemy" is sympathetic.

## Chapter 4: 天崩·陨落 (Celestial Fall)

### "The heavens broke. We are still falling."

The fourth fragment is embedded in **云霄城 (Cloud Zenith City)** — a floating city of immortals that has been slowly falling from the sky ever since the Furnace shattered. The city's inhabitants, once enlightened cultivators, have degenerated into **堕仙 (Fallen Immortals)** — beings who traded their humanity for power and are now trapped in grotesque, half-ascended forms.

The city is ruled by **堕仙·玄霄 (Fallen Immortal · Xuán Xiāo)** — a cultivator who was on the verge of true ascension when the Furnace broke. The shockwave froze him mid-transformation: half of his body is divine light, the other half is rotting flesh. His mind has shattered into fragments that manifest as separate entities throughout the city. He seeks to complete his ascension by consuming the Furnace fragment, unaware that it is the very thing preventing it.

**Key Story Beats:**
- Ascend through the city's falling districts via gravity-defying platforms
- Confront each of 玄霄's fragmented personalities as sub-bosses
- Recover 玄霄's lucid core-remnant, which preserves an unaltered record of the Shattering
- Face 玄霄's unified form atop the Celestial Spire as the city crumbles
- Decide whether 玄霄 completes ascension or returns his identity to mortal memory
- Absorb the fourth fragment, gain brief gravity manipulation, and receive the **炉心敕印 (Furnace Mandate)** that recognizes the player as a temporary mobile Furnace
- 云游 confesses that his reversal saved the living but caused the fracture; the Mandate opens the descent to 烬座

**Tone:** Tragic, epic, vertiginous. The ruins of paradise. The cost of ambition.

---

## Chapter 5: 烬座·归墟 (Throne of Ashes · Return to Void)

### "The last Ember burns at the end of all things."

The final descent. With four fragments absorbed, you enter the **烬座 (Throne of Ashes)** — the shattered core of the Celestial Furnace, now a realm of pure entropy where time, space, and identity dissolve. The souls of every being who has died since the Shattering drift here in an endless, silent current.

烛阴 awaits at the heart of the furnace, bound to his throne by the very chains the other Soul-Forgers used to imprison him. But he is more powerful than ever — the corruption he seeded in the other four realms has been feeding him strength. His dragon form fills the horizon, a living constellation of dying stars and Ember-light.

**Key Story Beats:**
- Traverse the Throne realm, where the geometry is non-Euclidean and time loops
- See the actual consequences of the four prior chapter choices; the player may accept or regret them but cannot rewrite them
- Commune with the nine fallen Soul-Forgers and confront the two surviving witnesses, 云游 and 寂灭
- Face 烛阴 in a multi-phase boss fight across cosmic scale
- **Ending Decision:** choose one of three base endings, or unlock a costly fourth path by completing the three major truth questlines

**Tone:** Cosmic, philosophical, final. The end of a journey. Questions without easy answers.

## Ending Paths

### Ending A: 薪火相传 (Passing the Flame)
Repair the Furnace. Your collected Embers reignite the cosmic fire. Souls resume their cycle. You dissolve into pure Ember, becoming the first soul to reincarnate through the restored Furnace. The world is reborn. You are not there to see it.

### Ending B: 守炉人 (Furnace-Keeper)
Take 烛阴's place. You bind yourself to the Throne of Ashes, becoming the new guardian of the cycle. Eternity stretches before you — silent, solitary, necessary. The realms heal slowly under your vigil. No one will ever know your name.

### Ending C: 大寂灭 (The Great Silence)
Shatter the Furnace completely. Without the cycle, souls are truly free for the first time — free to live, free to die, free to fade. No more reincarnation, no more cosmic order. The universe becomes a wilderness of possibility. Whether this is salvation or damnation... no one can say.

### Hidden Ending D: 共铸新炉 (The Shared Forging)
Return the Furnace memories to 烛阴 and convince him to help build a cycle that preserves less memory as fuel and allows souls to refuse reincarnation. The player and 烛阴 dissolve their individual identities into paired regulators so neither can control the new Furnace alone. This is the most hopeful ending, not a perfect one: the world gains consent and oversight, but loses both people who made it possible.

**Unlock requirement:** collect all four furnace memories (`furnace_memory_1..4`, found one per level in 5-1…5-4) and complete all three major Chapter 5 truth questlines — `灵魂的回归` (`quest_soul_return`), `铸魂者的最后一个问题` (`quest_forge_last_question`), and `天炉的低语` (`quest_furnace_whisper`). The first three endings are never morality-locked; prior choices alter dialogue, assistance, and epilogues instead.

## Story Themes

- **Cycle vs. End:** Is eternal recurrence a blessing or a prison?
- **Memory and Identity:** If you forget who you were, are you still that person?
- **Sacrifice:** Every ending, including the hidden fourth path, demands giving something up. There is no perfect resolution.
- **Chinese Cosmology:** Heaven (天), Earth (地), Underworld (冥), and Humanity (人) in cosmic balance.
- **The Value of Impermanence:** Things are precious *because* they end. 烛阴's flaw was refusing to accept this.
