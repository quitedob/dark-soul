# Chapter 3: 玉障·迷心 (Jade Veil · Lost Mind)

**Type:** Puzzle/Exploration Chapter
**Theme:** Illusion-Bound Jade Forest
**Tone:** Elegant, deceptive, melancholy
**Levels:** 6
**Boss:** 玉面狐·九尾 (Jade-Faced Fox · Nine-Tails)
**Fragment:** 幻烬 (Illusion Ember)

---

## Chapter Vision

翠微林 (Jade-Green Forest) was once a sacred grove where fox spirits and nature deities lived in harmony with mortal pilgrims. The Illusion Ember corrupted its guardian — a nine-tailed fox spirit — who now weaves the forest into an ever-shifting dreamscape. Travelers who enter see what they most desire or most fear, wandering in circles until they waste away.

The player must navigate environments where **nothing is quite what it seems**. Walls shift, paths loop, enemies appear as friends, and the forest itself seems to remember the player's past lives.

**Key Mechanic Introduction:** Illusion/reality duality, memory-based puzzles, alternate paths based on perception.

---

## Level Structure

### Level 3-1: 翠微林入口 (Jade Forest Entrance)
A deceptively peaceful forest entrance. Bamboo groves, gentle streams, the sound of wind chimes. But things are subtly wrong — the same tree appears three times, the stream flows uphill, your reflection in the water doesn't match your movements.

**Enemies:** 幻影蝶 (Illusion Butterfly) ×3 — small, harmless-looking, but their dust causes controller inversion for 5s

**Traps:** Loop paths (player walks straight but returns to start unless they notice the landmark trick), false floors over spike pits disguised as flower beds

**Loot:** 翠微玉佩 (Jade Forest Pendant), 带脉解锁石 (Belt Vessel Stone)

---

### Level 3-2: 记忆回廊 (Memory Corridor)
A narrow canyon where the walls are covered in **记忆苔 (Memory Moss)** — touching it replays fragments of past lives. The player experiences echoes of their previous incarnations as an Ember Scion, learning backstory through playable flashbacks.

**Enemies:** 记忆窃贼 (Memory Thief) ×4 — humanoid figures that drain Focus on hit; 回音灵 (Echo Spirit) ×2 — copies of the player's last attack pattern

**Puzzles:** Must identify which memories are real vs illusions. Real memories unlock doors; false ones spawn enemies.

**Loot:** 前世护符 (Past-Life Talisman), 灵气结晶×5

---

### Level 3-3: 狐嫁道 (Fox Wedding Road)
A hauntingly beautiful path where a phantom fox-spirit wedding procession passes through at regular intervals. The procession is an illusion — but the danger is real. Foxfire lanterns float along the path, and ghostly palanquin bearers march in slow, hypnotic rhythm.

**Enemies:** 狐火灯妖 (Foxfire Lantern Spirit) ×3 — floating, fire attacks; 嫁衣女鬼 (Wedding Gown Ghost) ×2 — appears during the procession, devastating if she catches you

**Puzzles:** Must follow the procession through the level without being seen — a stealth section. Lanterns must be extinguished in the correct order to open the exit.

**Loot:** 狐火之灯 (Foxfire Lantern — off-hand), 红盖头 (Red Bridal Veil — head armor)

---

### Level 3-4: 镜花水月亭 (Mirror-Flower Water-Moon Pavilion)
An elegant lakeside pavilion where the reflection in the lake shows a different version of the world. The player must use the lake's reflection to see the true path — what looks like water is solid (and vice versa), invisible platforms appear in the reflection, and hidden enemies are revealed.

**Enemies:** 水中月 (Water Moon) ×3 — enemies that exist in the reflection world, can only be hit when the player is on "reflection-revealed" platforms; 镜花精 (Mirror Flower Spirit) ×2

**Puzzles:** The central puzzle of the chapter. Navigate an area where reality and reflection are swapped. Step on the wrong surface → fall into the lake → reset.

**Loot:** 镜花簪 (Mirror Flower Hairpin), 阴跷脉解锁石 (Yin Heel Vessel Stone)

---

### Level 3-5: 九尾迷宫 (Nine-Tails Maze)
An ever-shifting hedge maze made of flowering jade vines. The maze's layout changes based on which direction the player faced when entering — each of the nine tails corresponds to a different maze configuration.

**Enemies:** 迷宫守卫 (Maze Guardian) ×4 — animate jade statues; 迷心妖狐 (Mind-Lost Fox Demon) ×2 — agile, illusion-casting

**Puzzles:** The Nine-Tails Riddle — at each intersection, a fox statue poses a riddle. Correct answer → path opens. Wrong answer → maze shifts, enemies respawn. Riddles are based on Chinese classical poetry and philosophy.

**Loot:** 九尾迷图 (Nine-Tails Maze Map — key item for boss), 纯阳石×3

---

### Level 3-6: 月华台 (Moonlit Terrace)
The heart of the forest. A circular terrace bathed in eternal moonlight, surrounded by nine blooming **幻花 (Illusion Flowers)** , one for each of Nine-Tails' tails. The fox spirit sits at the center in human form — an impossibly beautiful woman in jade-green robes. She does not attack immediately. She speaks first.

**Boss:** 玉面狐·九尾 (Jade-Faced Fox · Nine-Tails)

---

## Boss: 玉面狐·九尾

**Key Mechanics:**
- Phase 1 (100-70%): Nine-Tails fights defensively, testing the player. Light attacks, teleports, illusion clones.
- Phase 2 (70-30%): Each tail manifests as a separate illusion entity. The player must identify the real Nine-Tails among clones. Hitting a clone deals damage back to the player.
- Phase 3 (30-0%): Nine-Tails takes the form of someone from the player's past (narrative moment). The form shifts between all four classes' mentor figures. Mechanically, she gains access to abilities from the player's non-active classes.

**Unique Mechanic — Memory Gaze:**
At 50% HP, Nine-Tails freezes the fight and offers the player a vision: a playable flashback showing her corruption from gentle guardian to illusion-weaver. The player sees the Illusion Ember's arrival and the gradual twisting of her purpose. This is not skippable on first playthrough. On NG+, the player can interrupt it to skip to Phase 3 directly.
