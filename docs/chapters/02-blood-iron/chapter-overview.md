# Chapter 2: 血铁·战歌 (Blood & Iron · Warsong)

**Type:** Combat-Focused Chapter
**Theme:** Eternal War Fortress
**Tone:** Brutal, martial, honor-bound tragedy
**Levels:** 6
**Boss:** 血将军·刑天 (Blood General · Xíng Tiān)
**Fragment:** 战烬 (War Ember)

---

## Chapter Vision

铁啸关 (Iron Howl Pass) is a mountain fortress trapped in an eternal war. The War Ember fragment has cursed the garrison to fight, die, and rise again in an endless cycle. Two phantom armies clash daily across the fortress walls, their soldiers barely remembering why they fight — only that they must.

The player enters this nightmare to claim the fragment. Along the way, they'll navigate active battlefields, rescue imprisoned spirit-smiths, choose which army's territory to cross, and ultimately face the being whose rage fuels this endless war.

**Key Mechanic Introduction:** Weapon forging, siege warfare hazards, faction choice paths.

---

## Level Structure

### Level 2-1: 血染山道 (Blood-Stained Mountain Path)
The approach to Iron Howl Pass. A winding mountain trail littered with centuries of war detritus — broken siege weapons, rusted armor, mass graves marked by tattered battle flags.

**Enemies:** 失魂士兵 (Lost Soldiers) ×3, 弩炮残骸 (Ballista Wreck — stationary hazard)

**Traps:** Avalanche zones (sound-triggered), collapsed bridge (must find alternate route)

**Loot:** 督脉解锁石 (Governing Vessel Stone), 铁精×5

---

### Level 2-2: 铁啸关外墙 (Iron Howl Outer Wall)
The fortress's outer defenses. Ballista bolts fly overhead as phantom armies clash on the walls above. The player must navigate through an active (but not targeting them) battle.

**Enemies:** 失魂士兵 ×4, 战犬亡魂 (War Dog Wraith) ×2 — fast, low HP, hunt in pairs

**Traps:** Active ballista firing lanes (timed), burning oil cauldrons (can be tipped onto enemies), collapsing wall sections

**Loot:** 铁啸长矛 (Iron Howl Spear), 督脉升级材料×3

---

### Level 2-3: 俘虏营 (Prisoner Camp)
A fortified camp where spirit-smiths are held captive, forced to forge weapons for both armies. Freeing them unlocks weapon forging at future shrines.

**Enemies:** 守营鬼卒 (Camp Guard Wraith) ×3, 刑具精怪 (Torture Device Spirit) ×1 — stationary, AoE damage

**Puzzles:** Prisoner cages with key fragments scattered across camp. Collect all 3 fragments → free the smiths.

**Loot:** 铁心之锤 (Iron Heart's Hammer — key item for forging), 灵气结晶×3

---

### Level 2-4: 烽火台 (Beacon Tower)
A vertical climb through a signal tower. Each floor presents different combat scenarios. At the top, the player can light a beacon that changes enemy behavior in the next level (lights the way → fewer ambushes, but alerts the boss).

**Enemies:** 守殿武士 ×2, 烽火守魂 (Beacon Keeper Wraith) ×2 — ranged fire attacks

**Traps:** Collapsing stairs, fire spirit ambushes from unlit braziers

**Loot:** 烽火号角 (Beacon Horn — consumable, calls phantom ally for 60s), 纯阳石×2

---

### Level 2-5: 将军营帐 (General's Encampment)
刑天's war camp. The heart of the phantom army. Tattered tents, war tables with faded maps, and the lingering presence of the Blood General himself. This level introduces the chapter's primary puzzle: the **War Banner Puzzle**.

**Enemies:** 亲卫鬼将 (General's Guard) ×3 — elite, use formation tactics (flank + surround)

**Puzzles:** War Banner Puzzle — four banners must be raised in the correct order (matching the battles depicted in murals in 2-2 and 2-4). Wrong order spawns additional enemies.

**Loot:** 将军佩剑 (General's Ceremonial Sword), 战烬护符 (War Ember Talisman), 100 Embers

---

### Level 2-6: 刑天斗场 (Xíng Tiān Arena)
A vast circular arena carved into the mountain's peak. Two phantom armies fill the stands — not fighting each other, but watching. At the center stands 刑天, headless, massive, his twin axes embedded in the ground before him.

**Boss:** 血将军·刑天 (Blood General · Xíng Tiān)

*See full boss document in [bestiary/bosses-master.md](../../bestiary/bosses-master.md)*

- Phase 1: 刑天 fights with axes embedded (slower, deliberate)
