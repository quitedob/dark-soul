# Chapter 4: 天崩·陨落 (Celestial Fall)

**Type:** Epic/Platforming Chapter
**Theme:** Shattered Floating Immortal City
**Tone:** Tragic, epic, vertiginous
**Levels:** 6
**Boss:** 堕仙·玄霄 (Fallen Immortal · Xuán Xiāo)
**Fragment:** 天烬 (Sky Ember)

---

## Chapter Vision

云霄城 (Cloud Zenith City) was the pinnacle of mortal achievement — a floating metropolis where cultivators who neared immortality gathered to complete their ascension. When the Celestial Furnace shattered, the Sky Ember fragment embedded itself in the city's core, freezing every ascension in progress. The cultivators became **堕仙 (Fallen Immortals)** — half-divine, half-rotting, trapped in the exact moment of transformation for five centuries.

Now the city is slowly falling from the sky, its districts crumbling away one by one. The player must ascend through the ruins, confront the fragmented personalities of 玄霄, and claim the Sky Ember before the entire city collapses into the void.

**Key Mechanic Introduction:** Gravity manipulation (post-boss), vertical platforming, multiple sub-bosses (玄霄's personality fragments).

---

## Level Structure

### Level 4-1: 登天梯 (Stairway to Heaven)
The shattered staircase that once connected the mortal realm to Cloud Zenith City. Segments of stairs float in the void, some slowly rotating. The sky-scape is a permanent sunset — the last sunset the city ever saw.

**Enemies:** 梯卫亡魂 (Stairway Guard Wraith) ×3 — slow but heavy knockback attacks (knock player off edges); 云天鹰 (Cloud Sky Eagle) ×2 — flying, dive-bomb attacks

**Traps:** Collapsing stair segments, wind gusts that push the player toward edges

**Puzzles:** Rotating stair segments must be aligned using celestial dials positioned at anchor points

**Loot:** 登天符 (Heaven-Ascending Talisman), 阳跷脉解锁石 (Yang Heel Vessel Stone)

---

### Level 4-2: 炼丹云台 (Cloud Alchemy Platform)
A district dedicated to celestial alchemy. Massive bronze cauldrons float above eternal flames, refining elixirs that have been boiling for 500 years. The airborne elixir vapors cause hallucinations.

**Enemies:** 丹炉精怪 (Elixir Furnace Spirit) ×3 — stationary AoE damage + status clouds; 炼丹堕仙 (Alchemy Fallen Immortal) ×2 — slow spellcasters, throw volatile elixirs

**Traps:** Elixir vapor clouds (random status effect: slow, damage reversal, inverted controls), unstable floating platforms that sink when stepped on

**Puzzles:** Three celestial cauldrons must be fed correct ingredients (found in the level) to create the 清心丹 (Mind-Clearing Pill) that allows passage through a hallucination zone

**Loot:** 炼丹炉心 (Alchemy Furnace Core — crafting), 灵铁×5

---

### Level 4-3: 藏经阁 (Scripture Depository)
A vast floating library containing the accumulated knowledge of immortal cultivators. Bookshelves stretch into the clouds. Some books have become animate — **书精 (Book Spirits)** flutter through the air, their pages whispering forgotten techniques. A sealed copy of the unaltered Furnace record contains **玄霄残识**, the lucid core of 玄霄's divided mind; releasing it establishes the original account of the Shattering.

**Enemies:** 书精 (Book Spirit) ×5 — swarming, low HP but fast, steal items on hit (returned on kill); 守阁仙魂 (Library Guardian Spirit) ×2 — elite, uses techniques from random player classes

**Traps:** Trapped bookshelves (pull the wrong book → shelf collapses into spike trap), gravity anomalies (sections where gravity reverses)

**Puzzles:** The Library Index Puzzle — find four specific texts referenced in a central index. Each text contains part of a cultivation technique. Combine them to open the exit.

**Loot:** 天书法卷 (Celestial Book Scroll — spell), 阴维脉解锁石 (Yin Linking Vessel Stone), Talent Scroll ×1

---

### Level 4-4: 玄霄·嗔念台 (Xuán Xiāo's Wrath Platform)

**Sub-Boss Level.** The first of 玄霄's three fragmented personalities: **嗔念 (Wrath)** . An arena floating above the city where the Wrath fragment endlessly smashes pillars with bare fists.

**Boss:** 玄霄·嗔念 (Xuán Xiāo · Wrath Fragment)
- All-out aggression. No defensive AI — only attacks.
- High damage, fast, but predictable patterns
- Defeating it calms this fragment, revealing part of 玄霄's backstory

**Loot:** 嗔念之拳 (Wrath Fist — weapon), 龙鳞石×2

---

### Level 4-5: 玄霄·执念台 (Xuán Xiāo's Obsession Platform)

**Sub-Boss Level.** The second fragment: **执念 (Obsession)** . A replica of 玄霄's original cultivation chamber, perfectly preserved. The Obsession fragment endlessly repeats his ascension ritual, never completing it.

**Boss:** 玄霄·执念 (Xuán Xiāo · Obsession Fragment)
- Defensive, ritual-casting. Summons spirit guardians.
- Arena traps activate in sequence (the "ritual")
- Defeating it reveals 玄霄's history — he was attempting to ascend not for himself, but to save a dying loved one

**Loot:** 执念护符 (Obsession Talisman — accessory), 龙鳞石×2

---

### Level 4-6: 玄霄·真身·天顶 (Celestial Zenith — True Form)

The highest point of Cloud Zenith City. The Sky Ember fragment floats at the center of a crumbling celestial platform, and before it kneels **玄霄's true body** — half divine light, half rotting flesh, frozen mid-transformation for 500 years. As the player approaches, the defeated Wrath and Obsession fragments return to the body while the lucid **Core Remnant** recovered in 4-3 restores a third, temporary voice.

**Boss:** 堕仙·玄霄 (Fallen Immortal · Xuán Xiāo)

---

## Boss: 堕仙·玄霄

**Key Mechanics:**
- Phase 1 (100-60%): Divine half attacks with light-based magic (ranged, AoE beams). Rotting half is passive.
- Phase 2 (60-30%): Forms merge — attacks alternate between light and decay. Hits apply "Celestial Rot" — stacking DoT that also reduces max HP temporarily.
- Phase 3 (30-0%): 玄霄's mind fully fragments. The arena becomes a storm of three consciousnesses — the player must identify which attack pattern is currently active and respond. Wrath = aggressive, Obsession = ritual, Core = balanced.

**After Defeat:**
- At 10% HP, 玄霄残识 creates a lucid interval: choose **Complete Ascension** (`ch4_xuanxiao_fate=ascended`) or **Return to Memory** (`ch4_xuanxiao_fate=remembered`)
- Absorb the Sky Ember and gain brief gravity manipulation
- 玄霄残识 combines the Sky Ember with the unaltered record to produce the **炉心敕印 (Furnace Mandate)**
- 云游 confesses that his reversed soul-current saved the living but caused the five-way fracture
- The city begins its final collapse; the escape ends at the Mandate's descent gate to 烬座
- The story continues at `level_05_01`
