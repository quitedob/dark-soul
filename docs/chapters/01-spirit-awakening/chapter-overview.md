# Chapter 1: 灵墟·觉醒 (Spirit Ruins · Awakening)

**Type:** Tutorial Chapter
**Theme:** Abandoned Guardian Temple Complex
**Tone:** Mystery, loneliness, ancient duty
**Levels:** 5
**Boss:** 守炉灵·巨阙 (Furnace-Keeper · Giant Gate)
**Fragment:** 始烬 (First Ember)

---

## Chapter Vision

The player awakens in a crumbling temple that once served as the gateway to the Celestial Furnace. This chapter teaches all core mechanics through environmental design: combat basics, stamina management, Ember collection, checkpoint activation, dodge timing, parry windows, and combat style switching. The temple is inhabited by the remnants of its former guardians — now mindless husks repeating their ancient duties.

The tutorial is woven into the environment, not delivered through pop-ups. Ancient murals on the walls depict the correct techniques. Optional alcoves contain practice dummies. A gentle, mysterious voice (The Wandering Sage) offers occasional guidance at shrines.

---

## Level Structure

### Level 1-1: 苏醒之庭 (Courtyard of Awakening)

**Theme:** Overgrown temple entrance, moonlight, broken pillars
**Purpose:** Movement, camera, basic interaction

**Description:**
The player rises from a stone bier in a small shrine room. A dim Ember Shrine flickers nearby — interacting with it teaches checkpoint mechanics. The room opens into an overgrown courtyard with broken walls and moss-covered stones.

**Enemies:**
- 2x 失魂士兵 (Lost Soul Soldier) — slow, telegraphed, low damage. Teaches basic attack timing.

**Traps/Puzzles:**
- A cracked floor section that collapses if the player stands on it too long (teaches environmental awareness)

**Loot:**
- 破旧护符 (Worn Talisman) — first accessory, +5 max HP
- 50 Embers from enemies

**Key Lesson:** Movement, camera control, basic attack (light/heavy), interacting with shrines.

---

### Level 1-2: 守门廊 (Gate-Keeper's Corridor)

**Theme:** Narrow temple corridor with side alcoves
**Purpose:** Dodge timing, stamina management, parry introduction

**Description:**
A long corridor lined with collapsed statues. Side alcoves contain practice targets (non-hostile constructs) where the player can safely practice timing. Halfway through, the corridor narrows, forcing close combat.

**Enemies:**
- 3x 失魂士兵 (Lost Soul Soldier)
- 1x 守殿武士 (Temple Guardian Warrior) — stronger, uses heavy attacks. Teaches dodge/parry.

**Traps/Puzzles:**
- **火焰机关 (Flame Vent Trap):** Pressure plates trigger flame jets from wall carvings. Teaches watching for environmental tells (scorch marks on floor, faint hissing sound).
- **砸门陷阱 (Falling Gate):** A heavy stone gate drops if the player sprints through without pulling a visible lever first. Teaches observation before rushing.

**Loot:**
- 灵墟短剑 (Spirit Ruins Short Sword) — first weapon upgrade
- 30 Embers from enemies
- 回神香 (Spirit-Recalling Incense) — consumable, restores 30 Focus

**Key Lesson:** Dodge timing, stamina management, parry (optional alcove), trap awareness.

---

### Level 1-3: 明镜殿 (Hall of Mirrored Truth)

**Theme:** Reflection hall with mirrored walls, dim lighting
**Purpose:** Lock-on combat, enemy spacing, combat style switching

**Description:**
A temple hall where the walls are polished bronze mirrors. The reflections create confusion — enemies approach from multiple angles. A mural on the ceiling depicts the five combat styles. At the hall's center is a meditation circle where the player can freely switch between discovered combat styles.

**Enemies:**
- 3x 失魂士兵 (Lost Soul Soldier)
- 2x 镜中影 (Mirror Shade) — enemies that approach from mirror surfaces. Low HP, but they can emerge from any mirror. Teaches lock-on and situational awareness.

**Traps/Puzzles:**
- **镜光迷阵 (Mirror Light Puzzle):** Three mirrors must be rotated to direct a beam of light onto a sealed door. Teaches environmental manipulation.
- **碎镜陷阱 (Broken Mirror Trap):** Stepping on shattered mirror fragments makes noise, alerting enemies in adjacent rooms.

**Loot:**
- 战斗风格卷轴 (Combat Style Scroll) — unlocks the ability to switch styles at shrines
- 镜影斗篷 (Mirror-Shade Cloak) — light armor, +3% dodge invulnerability
- 60 Embers from enemies

**Key Lesson:** Lock-on targeting, managing multiple enemies, combat style introduction, puzzle mechanics.

---

### Level 1-4: 炼丹房遗迹 (Ruins of the Elixir Hall)

**Theme:** Destroyed alchemy laboratory, broken furnaces, spilled elixirs
**Purpose:** Environmental hazards, consumable use, boss preparation

**Description:**
The temple's alchemy wing, where the Furnace-Keepers once refined Embers into purified soul-stuff. Broken vats leak glowing liquid that damages on contact. The air shimmers with residual heat. This is the final preparation area before the boss.

**Enemies:**
- 2x 守殿武士 (Temple Guardian Warrior)
- 1x 炉渣怪 (Furnace Slag Beast) — a creature formed from congealed industrial waste. Slow, high HP, explodes on death (teaches watching for post-defeat threats)

**Traps/Puzzles:**
- **毒雾区 (Toxic Mist Zone):** Cracked pipes release damage-over-time mist. Must be bypassed by pulling levers to shut off valves.
- **不稳定地面 (Unstable Floor):** Sections of floor over old elixir pools are fragile — running across them causes them to break.
- **供物台 (Offering Pedestal):** A sealed door requires placing a specific item (found in the level) on a pedestal. Teaches key-item puzzles.

**Loot:**
- 炼丹师护腕 (Alchemist's Bracers) — arm armor, +5 Focus
- 炽火丸 (Blazing Fire Pill) ×2 — consumable, temporary +15% damage for 60s
- 守炉符文 (Furnace-Keeper's Rune) — key item for boss door
- 80 Embers from enemies

**Key Lesson:** Environmental hazards, consumable management, key-item puzzles, boss preparation.

---

### Level 1-5: 守炉殿·内廷 (Furnace-Keeper's Inner Sanctum)

**Theme:** Boss arena — the temple's sacred heart
**Purpose:** Boss encounter

**Description:**
A vast circular chamber at the temple's core. The ceiling has collapsed, revealing the night sky. At the center stands a massive stone construct — **巨阙 (Jù Quē)** — still performing its ancient duty: circling the inner sanctum, pausing at four watch-points, its ember-core pulsing with dying light. When the player approaches with the Furnace-Keeper's Rune, it recognizes you as an intruder and activates combat protocols.

**Boss: 守炉灵·巨阙 (Furnace-Keeper · Giant Gate)**

*See: [Chapter 1 Boss](bosses.md#守炉灵巨阙-furnace-keeper--giant-gate)*

**After Defeat:**
- The boss drops to 10% integrity and the Keeper Rune exposes its final command
- Choose **Release Duty** (`ch1_guardian_fate=released`) or **Preserve the Core** (`ch1_guardian_fate=preserved`)
- The boss body crumbles, revealing the 始烬 (First Ember) fragment
- Absorbing it stabilizes the first section of the player's incomplete 轮回印 and grants the first **天赋 (Talent Point)**
- The Wandering Sage appears in full and admits that the player was made to gather the fragments, but withholds who made them
- The First Ember projects a blood-stained military coordinate; the Keeper Rune opens the界门 to 铁啸关
- Chapter 1 completes and the story continues at `level_02_01`

## Tutorial Checklist

By the end of Chapter 1, the player should understand:

- [x] Basic movement and camera control
- [x] Light and heavy attacks
- [x] Dodge timing and invulnerability frames
- [x] Stamina management
- [x] Lock-on targeting and cycling
- [x] Ember collection and death recovery
- [x] Checkpoint (Ember Shrine) activation and resting
- [x] Combat style switching
- [x] Parry timing (optional practice)
- [x] Environmental hazard recognition
- [x] Puzzle solving (light mirrors, key items, levers)
- [x] Trap awareness (flame vents, unstable floors)
- [x] Boss combat fundamentals
