# Chapter 1: 灵墟·觉醒 — Levels Detail

## Level 1-1: 苏醒之庭 (Courtyard of Awakening)

### Layout
A small enclosed courtyard (20m × 15m) with:
- **Awakening Chamber:** Starting room with a stone bier and the first Ember Shrine
- **Courtyard:** Open area with broken pillars offering partial cover
- **Side Sanctum:** Small alcove with practice targets (non-hostile)
- **Exit Gate:** Leads to Level 1-2, opens after defeating both soldiers

### Enemy Placements
| Enemy | Position | Behavior |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 失魂士兵 A | Courtyard center | Patrols in a small loop, facing away from the Awakening Chamber on spawn |
| 失魂士兵 B | Near exit gate | Stationary, activates when player gets within 8m or Soldier A is engaged |

### Environmental Storytelling
- Faded murals on walls show the Celestial Furnace and the Soul-Forgers
- A shattered statue of a kneeling figure (pose resembles the player's waking animation)
- Fresh scratch marks on the exit gate — something tried to get in recently

### Pickups
- 破旧护符 (Worn Talisman): On the collapsed statue in the Side Sanctum
- 50 Embers: From enemies (25 each)

### Tutorial Moments
1. First Ember Shrine interaction → brief message: "烬龛的火焰回应了你" (The shrine's flame responds to you)
2. First enemy sighted → camera briefly pans to show the patrol route
3. First kill → Ember absorption animation, counter appears
4. Gate interaction prompt → teaches interaction button

## Level 1-2: 守门廊 (Gate-Keeper's Corridor)

### Layout
A 40m-long corridor, 4m wide, with:
- **Entrance Hall:** Wide area with four alcoves (two per side) containing practice dummies
- **Main Corridor:** Narrow passage with collapsed statues and wall carvings
- **Trap Section:** Three pressure plates connected to flame vents, with scorch marks visible
- **Gate Chamber:** Area before the exit with a suspended stone gate and a visible lever

### Enemy Placements
| Enemy | Position | Behavior |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 失魂士兵 A | First alcove (left) | Emerges when player passes the alcove |
| 失魂士兵 B | Mid-corridor | Patrols across the corridor width |
| 失魂士兵 C | Near flame trap area | Stationary, blocks the path — must be fought in trap zone |
| 守殿武士 | Gate Chamber | Stationary guarding the exit lever, heavy attacks only |

### Traps
**Flame Vent Trap (three plates):**
- Plate 1: triggers immediately, flame jet from right wall (3s duration, 15 dmg/s)
- Plate 2: 1s delay, flame jet from left wall
- Plate 3: triggers plates 1 AND 2 simultaneously
- Scorch marks visible before each plate (environmental tell)

**Falling Gate:**
- If the player crosses the gate threshold without pulling the lever, the gate drops (instant death if under it)
- The lever is visible from the Gate Chamber entrance, on the right wall
- Pulling the lever raises the gate permanently

### Pickups
- 灵墟短剑 (Spirit Ruins Short Sword): In the second right-side alcove, on a weapon rack
- 回神香 (Spirit-Recalling Incense): Behind the Gate Chamber, near the lever
- 30 Embers: From enemies

## Level 1-3: 明镜殿 (Hall of Mirrored Truth)

### Layout
A square hall (25m × 25m) with:
- **Entry Vestibule:** Small room with the Combat Style Scroll on a pedestal
- **Main Hall:** Bronze mirror walls on three sides, pillars at intervals
- **Central Circle:** Meditation platform where style switching is taught
- **Mirror Puzzle Room:** Separate chamber with three rotatable mirrors and a light beam
- **Exit:** Sealed door opened by solving the mirror puzzle

### Enemy Placements
| Enemy | Position | Behavior |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 失魂士兵 A | Main Hall, left side | Patrols |
| 失魂士兵 B | Main Hall, right side | Patrols |
| 镜中影 A | Emerges from north mirror | Only emerges from mirrors when player's back is turned |
| 镜中影 B | Emerges from east mirror | Only emerges when player is engaged with other enemies |
| 守殿武士 (optional) | Mirror Puzzle Room | Guards the puzzle — can be bypassed by solving puzzle from a distance |

### The Mirror Light Puzzle
Three rotatable mirrors (can be turned by interacting):
1. **Left Mirror:** Directs light beam rightward
2. **Center Mirror:** Receives and redirects beam
3. **Right Mirror:** Final mirror, must direct beam onto the door's seal

**Solution:** Left → aimed at Center (rotate 2 clicks right). Center → aimed at Right (rotate 1 click left). Right → aimed at door seal (rotate 3 clicks right).

A mural on the ceiling shows the correct angles (hint for observant players).

### Pickups
- 战斗风格卷轴 (Combat Style Scroll): Entry Vestibule pedestal
- 镜影斗篷 (Mirror-Shade Cloak): Hidden behind the north mirror after defeating both Mirror Shades
- 镜影刃 (Mirror-Shade Edge): Secret — break all mirrors after solving the puzzle (weapon appears in center)
- 60 Embers: From enemies

## Level 1-4: 炼丹房遗迹 (Ruins of the Elixir Hall)

### Layout
A multi-room complex with:
- **Entry:** Broken double doors, spilled glowing liquid on the floor
- **Main Hall:** Large room with broken furnaces, cracked vats, and toxic mist vents
- **Valve Room A:** Left side chamber with first shut-off valve (patrolled)
- **Valve Room B:** Right side chamber with second shut-off valve (patrolled)
- **Storage:** Small room with consumables, guarded by 炉渣怪
- **Offering Chamber:** Final room with sealed door and a pedestal

### Enemy Placements
| Enemy | Position | Behavior |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 守殿武士 A | Valve Room A | Guards the first valve |
| 守殿武士 B | Valve Room B | Guards the second valve |
| 炉渣怪 | Storage Room | Stationary until approached, explodes on death (3m radius, 30 damage) |
| 失魂士兵 A | Main Hall | Patrols through toxic mist (takes no damage from it) |

### Environmental Hazards
- **Toxic Mist:** Persistent AoE in Main Hall. 8 dmg/s while standing in it. Can be disabled by pulling both valves.
- **Unstable Floor (×3 sections):** Crack in Storage Room floor. Running causes break — player falls into shallow pit (10 damage) and must climb out. Walking is safe.
- **Glowing Liquid Pools:** Static pools that deal 20 dmg/s on contact. Cannot be disabled.

### Offering Puzzle
The sealed door in the Offering Chamber requires placing the **守炉符文 (Furnace-Keeper's Rune)** on the pedestal:
- The rune is located in the Storage Room, inside a broken furnace
- An inscription near the door reads: "Only the Keeper's mark opens the inner sanctum"
- Once placed, the door grinds open permanently

### Pickups
- 炼丹师护腕 (Alchemist's Bracers): Valve Room B, on a skeletal alchemist
- 炽火丸 (Blazing Fire Pill) ×2: Storage Room shelves
- 守炉符文 (Furnace-Keeper's Rune): Storage Room, inside broken furnace
- 守炉之誓 (Furnace-Keeper's Vow) prayer scroll: Secret alcove behind a breakable wall in Valve Room A
- 80 Embers: From enemies

## Level 1-5: 守炉殿·内廷 (Furnace-Keeper's Inner Sanctum)

### Layout
Boss arena. See [bosses.md](bosses.md) for full details.

### Pre-Boss Shrine
An Ember Shrine is located just outside the boss door. The Wandering Sage speaks here for the first time in a longer dialogue:
*"That construct still guards the inner sanctum. Five hundred years, and it has never stopped. Never questioned. Never rested. There is a kind of nobility in that... and a kind of tragedy. You carry the Keeper's Rune. It will see you as an intruder. Be ready."*

### Boss Door
A massive stone door with the Furnace-Keeper's seal. Using the rune causes the seal to glow, then shatter. The door opens slowly — a dramatic moment giving the player time to apply consumables.

### Post-Boss
- Absorbing 始烬 triggers a vision of the other four fragments
- The Wandering Sage appears fully, explains the quest structure
- Talent system unlocks
- The Ember Shrine activates as a fast-travel point
