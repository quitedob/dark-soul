# 烬渊 — Spells & Prayers Compendium (法术与祷告图鉴)

## Magic System Overview

烬渊 features two types of Focus-based abilities:

### 法术 (Spells) — Intelligence/Daoist Magic
Channeled through 法印 (Spell Seals), Staves, or Spell Swords. Based on Taoist five-element theory and esoteric cultivation arts. Primarily offensive and utility.

### 祷告 (Prayers) — Faith/Buddhist Invocation
Channeled through 念珠 (Prayer Beads), Talismans, or Ritual Tools. Based on Chinese Buddhist and folk religious practices. Primarily healing, buffing, and spirit-related.

## Design Philosophy

1. **No redundant spells** — every spell/prayer has a distinct tactical niche
2. **Sidegrade, not upgrade** — later spells are not strictly better; they offer different capabilities
3. **Chapter theme integration** — each chapter's spells reflect its elemental and thematic identity
4. **Resource tension** — powerful spells cost significant Focus, forcing strategic choices
5. **Cultural authenticity** — spell names and effects are rooted in actual Chinese magical/religious traditions
